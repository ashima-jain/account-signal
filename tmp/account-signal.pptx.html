What it does

Compresses 4 hours of account research into 30 seconds — then tells you what to do next.

5 Core Ideas

  • Evidence is the spine — everything cites it
  • FACT / HYPOTHESIS / UNKNOWN — server-enforced labels
  • 8-signal champion test — pure function, not gut feel
  • Deterministic NBA — deal-stage weighted tiers
  • Auto-seeding — Claude web search on account creation

The key principle

The model's job is research — thesis generation, web search.

The code's job is judgment — FACT invariant, champion tier, NBA, staleness.

The AI proposes; the code verifies.
